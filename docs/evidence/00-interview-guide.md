# Interview Guide

**Project:** VeriTalk
**Format:** Semi-structured interviews, Oct. 18–21, 2026. Notes are in [interview-notes.md](interview-notes.md).
**Objective:** Understand current fraud defense mechanisms, pain points, and user expectations for an AI-based risk assessment tool.

## Questions

1. How frequently do you or your family members encounter fraudulent or scam phone calls?
2. What tools or methods do you currently use to identify or block these calls?
3. What are the main pain points or frustrations with your current approach?
4. How do you usually determine if a caller is trying to deceive you during an active conversation?
5. Would you use an application that analyzes the *content* of the conversation in real-time to warn you?
6. How would you feel about putting a suspicious call on speakerphone so the app can analyze it?
7. What concerns do you have regarding privacy and the recording of your phone calls?
8. What features would a perfect scam-detection solution need to have for you to trust and use it?
